<Warning title="There are two things called &#x22;CAD&#x22;">
  CAD (*Computer-Aided Design*) actually means two totally different tools!

  - **Board CAD:** where you draw circuits and design PCBs. (The Cad in KiCad!)
  - **3D CAD**: where you design objects, like the case your board lives in.

  However, most of the time, people are referring to 3D CAD when they say CAD!
</Warning>
