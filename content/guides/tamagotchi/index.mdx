A Tamagotchi you designed yourself: your own circuit board with a screen,
three buttons, and a buzzer, in a case you modeled and printed. This guide
walks the whole journey - schematic → PCB → fabrication → enclosure - and
by the end you'll know KiCad well enough to design boards that don't exist
yet.

<Warning title="Make it yours">
Your board should NOT look like this one at the end. This is the base
Tamagotchi - you're expected to add your own components and switch it up
to personalize it. Spice it up! Add some flavor!
</Warning>

<PartsList />

The XIAO-ESP32-C3 is the MCU - the tiny computer that runs your code and
drives everything else. It wins here because it's affordable, has WiFi and
BLE built in, and includes onboard LiPo charging, so no external charger
chip is needed. (If you don't need wireless, the RP2040 is a great pick
with excellent documentation; the nRF52840 is the go-to for low-power BLE.)

Design is an iterative process - the original build took 20+ hours of
adjusting to look this pretty. That's normal, and you're not alone:
the #fallout channel on the Hack Club Slack is there when you're stuck.
