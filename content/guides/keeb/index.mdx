A fully functional mechanical keyboard - custom PCB, custom case, custom
firmware - from scratch. If you can hold a soldering iron, you can do
this. The guide covers everything from schematic to typing.

We suggest a **60% or 65% layout** if this is your first time.

<PartsList />

<Warning title="Hot iron, real fumes">
Solder in a ventilated space and put the iron in its stand every time it
leaves your hand. If you've never soldered before, spend fifteen minutes
with <Tool slug="soldering-iron" /> first - this guide assumes the
basics.
</Warning>

## What you'll build

- **Custom PCB** designed for this project
- **Case & plate** - everything to house your board
- **Your choice of switches** - linear, tactile, or clicky
- **A clean, no-nonsense keycap set**
- **A USB-C cable** to connect to your machine

Then Add your own twist if you want! The crazier, the better. Make it a
weird shape, or add a cool feature, or silkscreen! The point is that you
know what you want to make, and you can change this along the way.
